About Me

Robotics and embedded systems engineer, hardware-first, currently in Munich

Hello — I'm Ajay Mannolkar, a Mechatronics and Cyber-Physical Systems master's student at Deggendorf Institute of Technology. I started in a polytechnic workshop in Belagavi, wiring servo controllers into a full-size humanoid robot with eight classmates. It won second prize at a national event, and it set the pattern for everything since: learn the hardware by building the hardware.

After my bachelor's in mechanical engineering I founded Technolkar Innovations, making DIY electronics kits for students. I ran the whole loop myself — circuit design in Proteus, firmware in the Arduino IDE, testing, soldering, assembly, packaging, documentation, the YouTube channel that marketed it, and the workshops that taught students to use the kits.

My work spans hardware and simulation: embedded C on ESP32 and Arduino, LIDAR and ultrasonic sensing on mobile robots, Pixhawk flight control on a field drone, and most recently a VR driver-training simulator at THD where I led a seven-person team and built the pedestrian and traffic system. I am currently learning ROS 2 and rebuilding my warehouse robot's navigation stack on it.

I am looking for a master's thesis placement or internship in robotics or embedded systems, in or around Munich.
