02 — Selected work

Projects

01
THD Germany · 2024–25

Driver-Perspective Active Safety System

Repository

A four-level VR driving trainer built in Unity — day, night, fog and rain conditions, autonomous emergency braking and ABS, crash detection with logged reaction times, and an inverse-kinematics driver whose hands track the wheel. Runs on a Meta Quest 3 with a HORI racing wheel and pedals. Seven-person team at THD; I led it.

My part was the pedestrian and traffic system. Mixamo characters rigged with NavMesh agents, footpaths baked as the only walkable surface, an AI spawner cloning pedestrians across the city, and a wander script sending each to random footpath destinations — so the streets fill with people who distract the driver without ever stepping into the road.

Project paper
Unity 3D · C# · NavMesh · Mixamo · Meta Quest 3 · HORI wheel
