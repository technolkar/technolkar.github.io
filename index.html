About

Background

I started in a polytechnic workshop in Belagavi, wiring servo controllers into a humanoid robot with eight classmates. It won second prize at a national event, and it set the pattern for everything since: learn the hardware by building the hardware.

After my bachelor's in mechanical engineering I founded Technolkar Innovations, making DIY electronics kits for students. I ran the whole loop — circuit design in Proteus, firmware in the Arduino IDE, testing, soldering, assembly, packaging, documentation, the YouTube channel that marketed it, and the workshops. Fifty-plus kits and two hundred-plus trained students later I paused it: the market for that product was not there, and I wanted the theory behind what I had been building by instinct.

So I came to Germany. At THD I work on cyber-physical systems — most recently leading a seven-person team on a VR driver-training simulator, where I built the pedestrian and traffic system.

I am looking for a master's thesis placement or internship in robotics or embedded systems, in or around Munich.
